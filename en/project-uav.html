04.1Summary

The goal was to design a flight controller from scratch instead of buying one, solving power, sensing and motor drive on a single board. The board's arms are also the frame: the motors sit in the mounts at the ends of the arms.

The ESP32's two cores split the work: one runs sensor reads and the PID loop, the other handles communication with the ground station.

04.3Hardware

MCUESP32-WROOM-32 (dual-core, 240 MHz)
IMUMPU-6050 · I²C (SDA IO21, SCL IO22)
Motor drive4 × SI2302 N-MOSFET, low-side; 1N4148W flyback diode and 1 µF capacitor on each motor
Motor pinsMOT_1 IO4 · MOT_2 IO33 · MOT_3 IO32 · MOT_4 IO25
Power1S Li-Po; charged from USB-C via TP4056; MIC5219 3.3 V LDO; AO3401 for USB/battery path selection
ProgrammingCP2102N USB-UART with auto reset/boot circuit
OtherBattery voltage sense (IO35), on/off circuit, 3 debug LEDs, expansion header (UART2, I²C)

04.4Software

  • Core 0: MPU-6050 reads, accelerometer/gyro fusion with a complementary filter, roll/pitch/yaw PID loop.
  • Core 1: control input and telemetry over ESP-NOW (or UDP over Wi-Fi).

04.5Status

  • Schematic and PCB design
  • PID stabilisation and complementary filter
  • GPS-based autonomous waypoint following (in development)
